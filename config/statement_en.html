The Game

This game is based on the board game TZAAR, invented by Kris Burm. There are no deviations from the official rules, except for the colors of the pieces (Red and Blue instead of White and Black).

  Goal

Win the game by eliminating all of your opponent's pieces of a specific type (tzaar, tzarra, or tott) or by making them unable to perform a valid capture on their turn.

  Rules

TZAAR is played on a hexagonal board with 60 spaces (there is no playable space in the center).
Each player controls 30 pieces: 6 tzaars, 9 tzarras, and 15 totts.
The game uses a fixed starting arrangement. Player 0 (Red) plays first.

Coordinates:
The board uses an (X, Y) coordinate system with both coordinates ranging from 0 to 8. X-coordinates increase from bottom-left to top-right, and Y-coordinates increase from top to bottom. For easy reference, the coordinates of every space are shown in the initial board setup diagram below.

Game Phases:
Your code will be called twice in a row during your turn, corresponding to the two phases:
  • Phase 1: You must capture an opponent's piece. If you cannot, you lose.
  • Phase 2: You can either capture another opponent's piece, reinforce one of your own pieces, or pass by outputting WAIT.
Note: On the first turn of the game, Player 0 (Red) plays Phase 1 only and skips Phase 2.
Victory and defeat are checked immediately after each phase: as soon as a player has no pieces left of one type, the game ends at once, even in the middle of a turn.

Movement & Capture:
Pieces move in a straight line across any number of empty spaces. Pieces cannot move across the center of the board. They cannot jump over other pieces and cannot land on an empty space.
When capturing, your stack must be of equal or greater height than the opponent's stack. The captured stack is removed, and the attacking stack moves onto the space.
When reinforcing, you move your stack onto another of your own stacks. The heights are added, and the top piece determines the type of the new stack. If this removes your last remaining piece of a type from the board, you immediately lose the game.
Victory Conditions
  • You capture your opponent's last tzaar, tzarra, or tott.
Defeat Conditions
  • You lose all pieces of one type (tzaar, tzarra, or tott).
  • You cannot make a valid capture during Phase 1.
  • Your program does not provide a valid command in the given time limit.

  Game Protocol

Initialization input
Line 1: An integer myId indicating your player ID (0 or 1).
Input for one game turn
Line 1: An integer actionPhase (1 or 2).

Line 2: An integer pieceCount the number of stacks on the board.

Next pieceCount lines: 5 space-separated integers: x y owner type height.
  • x: X coordinate of the stack
  • y: Y coordinate of the stack
  • owner: owner of the stack (0 or 1)
  • type: type of the top piece of the stack (0 = tzaar, 1 = tzarra, 2 = tott)
  • height: height of the stack
Output for one game turn
A single line for your action: MOVE x1 y1 x2 y2 to move from (x1, y1) to (x2, y2), or WAIT (only valid in Phase 2).
Constraints
Response time for first turn ≤ 1000 ms
Response time for one turn ≤ 50 ms

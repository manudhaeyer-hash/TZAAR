The Game

This game is based on the board game TZAAR, invented by Kris Burm. There are no deviations from the official rules, except for the colors of the pieces (Red and Blue instead of White and Black).

  Goal

Win the game by eliminating all of your opponent's pieces of a specific type (TZAAR, TZARRA, or TOTT) or by making them unable to perform a valid capture on their turn.

  Rules

TZAAR is played on a hexagonal board with 60 spaces (4 rings around an empty center).
Each player controls 30 pieces: 6 TZAARs, 9 TZARRAs, and 15 TOTTs.
The game uses a fixed starting arrangement. Player 0 (Red) plays first.

Coordinates:
The board uses an (X, Y) coordinate system from 0 to 8. The X axis represents the diagonal from top-left to bottom-right, and the Y axis represents the horizontal rows from top to bottom. The leftmost space on the board is at (0, 4), and the topmost spaces are on the row Y = 0 from X = 4 to X = 8.

Game Phases:
Your code will be called twice in a row during your turn, corresponding to the two phases:
  • Phase 1: You must capture an opponent's piece. If you cannot, you lose.
  • Phase 2: You can either capture another opponent's piece, reinforce one of your own pieces, or pass by outputting WAIT.
Note: At the very beginning of the game, Player 0 (Red) only plays once (they only play Phase 1).

Movement & Capture:
Pieces move in straight lines across any number of empty spaces. They cannot jump over other pieces and cannot land on an empty space.
When capturing, your stack must be of equal or greater height than the opponent's stack. The opponent's stack is then removed, and yours replaces it.
When reinforcing, you move your stack onto another of your own stacks. The heights are added, and the top piece determines the type of the new stack.
Victory Conditions
  • You capture your opponent's last TZAAR, TZARRA, or TOTT.
  • Your opponent cannot make a valid capture during Phase 1.
Defeat Conditions
  • You lose all pieces of one type (TZAAR, TZARRA, or TOTT).
  • You cannot make a valid capture during Phase 1.
  • Your program does not provide a valid command in the given time limit.

  Game Protocol

Initialization input
Line 1: An integer myId indicating your player ID (0 or 1).
Input for one game turn
Line 1: An integer actionPhase (1 or 2).

Line 2: An integer validPieceCount the number of stacks on the board.

Next validPieceCount lines: 5 space-separated integers: x y owner type height.
  • x: X coordinate of the space
  • y: Y coordinate of the space
  • owner: owner of the stack (0 or 1)
  • type: type of the top piece of the stack (0 = TOTT, 1 = TZARRA, 2 = TZAAR)
  • height: height of the stack
Output
A single line for your action: MOVE x1 y1 x2 y2 to move from (x1, y1) to (x2, y2), or WAIT (only valid in Phase 2).
Constraints
Response time for first turn ≤ 1000 ms
Response time for one turn ≤ 50 ms
